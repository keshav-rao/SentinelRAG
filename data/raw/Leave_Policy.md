# Sentinel Global — Employee Leave & Time-Off Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2024-002  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Owner:** Human Resources Department  

---

## 1. Overview
Sentinel Global is committed to supporting work-life balance while ensuring operational continuity. This document defines employee entitlement to statutory and discretionary leaves.

---

## 2. Categories of Leave

### 2.1 Privilege / Earned Leave (PL)
* **Accrual:** Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of Privilege Leave per completed calendar month of active service (total 18 days per annum).
* **Accumulation & Carry Forward:** Employees may carry forward accrued PL year-on-year up to a hard ceiling of **45 days**.
* **Lapse Rule:** Any accrued PL exceeding 45 days as of December 31st automatically lapses without financial encashment.
* **Encashment:** Encashment is permissible only upon formal resignation or retirement, calculated against base monthly salary.

### 2.2 Casual Leave (CL)
* Entitlement: **12 days** per calendar year credited on January 1st.
* CL is intended for personal contingencies and short notice absences.
* Maximum consecutive CL allowed is 3 days.
* Unused CL cannot be carried forward to the subsequent calendar year and lapses on December 31st.

### 2.3 Sick & Medical Leave (SL)
* Entitlement: **10 days** per calendar year.
* Any consecutive sick leave exceeding **3 continuous working days** mandates a registered medical practitioner's fitness certificate submitted to HR within 48 hours of return.

### 2.4 Parental Leaves
* **Maternity Leave:** 26 continuous weeks of fully paid leave for female employees for up to two surviving children.
* **Paternity Leave:** 10 working days of fully paid leave, valid within 6 months following childbirth.
