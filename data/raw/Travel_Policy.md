# Sentinel Global — Corporate Travel & Expense Policy

**Document ID:** POL-OPS-2024-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Owner:** Global Operations & Finance Committee  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy outlines the authorized expense limits, booking guidelines, and reimbursement procedures for Sentinel Global employees undertaking business travel. All domestic and international trips must be pre-approved by the respective Department Head via the Sentinel ERP portal.

---

## 2. Accommodation & Lodging Guidelines

### 2.1 Hotel Accommodation Limits
Employees travelling for approved business purposes can claim hotel accommodation up to **₹3,000 per night** (taxes inclusive). 

* **Uniform Cap:** This limit of ₹3,000 per night applies uniformly across all domestic travel locations, cities, and regions. No exceptions or higher city tiers (including metropolitan zones such as Mumbai, Delhi NCR, or Bengaluru) are authorized under standard travel limits. Any booking exceeding ₹3,000 per night requires prior written sign-off from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
* **Booking Window:** Employees must book lodging at least 7 days prior to departure through the corporate travel desk.

### 2.2 Shared Accommodation
When two employees of the same gender travel together on company assignment, they are encouraged to share twin-occupancy rooms with an enhanced combined cap of ₹4,500 per night.

---

## 3. Daily Per Diem & Meals
Employees travelling outstation are entitled to a daily meal allowance of up to **₹800 per day**.
* Itemized tax invoices (GSTIN compliant) must accompany every claim.
* Alcohol, minibar charges, and personal laundry services are strictly non-reimbursable.

---

## 4. Transportation & Commute

### 4.1 Air Travel
* **Domestic:** Economy class only for flights under 6 hours duration.
* **International:** Economy class for flights under 8 hours; Premium Economy for flights exceeding 8 hours with VP approval.

### 4.2 Local City Transit
* Employees may claim app-based taxi rides (e.g., Uber / Ola) up to **₹500 per day** for direct transit between hotel, client offices, and transit hubs.
* Auto-rickshaw and public metro expenses are fully reimbursable upon submitting valid receipts or transit cards statement.

---

## 5. Expense Submission & Reimbursement Deadlines
All travel expense reports must be submitted within **30 days** of the conclusion of the business trip.
* Claims submitted after 30 days will be automatically rejected by the automated payroll engine.
* Original digital or scanned receipts must be uploaded for any item exceeding ₹100.
